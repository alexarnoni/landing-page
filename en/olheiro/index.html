// Brasileirão Série A

Olheiro

Team performance analysis and talent identification for the Brazilian Série A.

Open Platform View on GitHub

Problem

Analyze the performance of Série A teams and identify players who get little visibility but strong numbers.

Data

The dashboard uses data synced through the SportDB API. The platform tracks 20 teams and more than 500 catalogued players.

Technical decisions

  • Garimpo is the model that rates players by position, using per-90-minute metrics.
  • It highlights players with a high score and few minutes played, which is where talent tends to go unnoticed.
